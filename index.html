          Plain English explanation
          
          
          
          
            
            
          
          
          
          Two ways to get a plain English explanation. Explain with Daybook Assistant sends the letter (its text, or up to 8 page photos) to Daybook Assistant, Daybook's AI helper, which runs on Claude, an AI made by Anthropic, a US company. You do not need an AI account of your own. The explanation is saved with this letter, and you can change or delete it. Anthropic does not train its models on it, and the bridge keeps only a count of words used. Send to my AI app opens the share sheet for Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot or Gemini; paste the reply below. Either way the letter leaves Daybook and goes to an AI company's servers, so leave out anything you would not want sent. AI can get things wrong: check anything that matters with your care team.
          
          
          
          
          This explanation is for context only. It is not medical advice. Always check changes with the medical team.

They say what a food is like, not how much was eaten. A guide for the conversation, not medical advice.

          Two A4 pages to hand over or email: what is new, eight readings at a glance, your medicines and your questions with room to write the answers, then the charts and the notes week by week. Every figure comes from what was logged. Preview shows the two pages and prints them; Send to the team puts them in an email from your own Mail app, for a contact with an email address. Summarise with Daybook Assistant sends these notes and your questions to Daybook Assistant (AI from Anthropic, in the US) to write each week up in a few lines and tidy the spelling; without it the report shows the most telling note of each day. Suggest questions to ask sends them too, and offers questions you can add with one tap. Send or copy does the same through your own AI app. Any of these sends the notes to an AI company's servers, so leave out anything you would not want sent.
          
        
          Questions for the team
          
        
        
          One recording for the whole appointment. Tap a question's number as they start answering it, so each answer is easy to find later.
          
        
          
        
          
        
          Summary
          
        
          Simple checks on the readings by the app, worst first. Not medical advice. Anything that worries you, call the team.
          
        
          
        
          Notes
          
        
          Week by week, seven days at a time back from the last day: the longest written notes, and how many more are in Daybook, or each week in a few lines once Daybook Assistant has summarised them (it can miss or misread things).

          Food
          
        
          
          
          
          Adds typical portion sizes to the Food sheet, and rough calorie, protein, carb and fat figures to Today, the Food diary and Notes for the team. Estimates only, shared by everyone on this account, not medical advice.
          
          
          
          Each day's estimated protein is compared with it in the Food diary and in Notes for the team.
          
        
          
        
          Notes for the team
          
        
          
          
          
          Printed at the top of the two-page Notes for the team, for example your full name. Left empty, the report uses the first name of the household's patient.
          
        
          
        
          Reminders
          
        
          
          
          
          
          
          
            
            
          
          
          
          
          
          One notification, no pressure. Needs reminders on for at least one phone in the household.
